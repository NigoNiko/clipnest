## 繁體中文

### 調整內容

- 設定頁重新整理為「外觀」「個人化」「資料與隱私」「關於」，開關靠近對應選項。
- 介面語言切換後立即生效，英文標題與按鈕的大小寫更一致。
- 移除「忽略指定應用程式」；需要暫時停止收集時，可使用「暫停記錄」。

- 寬螢幕上的預覽卡片適度加寬，卡片與收藏夾的選取邊框保持一致。

### 問題修正

- 過長的網頁標題最多顯示兩行並以省略號結尾，過長網址維持單行顯示。
- 滑鼠停留在檔名時，會以白底圓角提示框顯示完整名稱，修正黑色提示框與形狀異常。
- 修正設定視窗頂部透出背景文字的問題。

## English

### Changes

- Settings are organized into Appearance, Personalization, Data & Privacy, and About, with switches beside their options.
- Language changes take effect immediately. English headings and buttons use consistent capitalization.
- Removed Ignored Apps. Use Pause Recording when you want to stop capturing clipboard items temporarily.
- The main window opens automatically on the first launch after installation or an update.
- Preview cards are slightly wider on large displays, and cards and favorites use matching selection outlines.
- Site icons sit on white rounded tiles, including light colored icons.

### Fixes

- Long page titles show up to two lines with an ellipsis. Long web addresses stay on one line.
- Hovering a filename shows its full name in a rounded white tooltip, fixing the dark and misshapen popup.
- Fixed background text showing through the top of the Settings window.
